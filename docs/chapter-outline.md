## Threads running through the whole book

These aren't single chapters — weave them wherever they fit naturally:

- **The power of connections** — most career opportunities came through
  people, not applications.
- **Crossroads and fate** — university choice, meeting his wife, country
  moves: small decisions that changed everything.
- **Technology transforming industries** — why many companies he worked for
  no longer exist (closed or merged), and what that says about change.
- **Culture and leadership** — organisational culture and values, tested
  most directly while leading through the pandemic.
- **Career assessments** — decades of personality/strengths tests as a
  reflective thread on why he moved between industries, companies and
  countries (possible chapter: "What the Tests Said About Me — and Whether
  They Were Right").

## Life Timeline Map

Before recording anything, sketch one page with 10–15 major markers, e.g.:

```
1954 – Born
1965 – School years begin to shape interests
1972 – University decision
1976 – First career step
1982 – Move overseas (Canada)
1985 – Marriage
1990s – Major career development
2000s – Leadership roles
2020 – Leading during Covid
Today – Reflection and legacy
```

This becomes the spine for scheduling interview sessions and organising
material in `archive/`.
