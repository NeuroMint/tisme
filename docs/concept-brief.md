# Concept Brief — Family Legacy Platform

*For Graham and Tom Perry. Working concept, not a commitment.*

## Purpose

A digital platform that lets individuals and families preserve their life
stories, values, memories and family history for future generations —
combining autobiography, genealogy, multimedia archives and (eventually) AI
memory preservation into one private family archive that grows over time.

The project starts as a pilot: **Graham Perry's own life story and family
archive.** That pilot serves two purposes — produce Graham's autobiography
for the family, and act as the proof of concept for a platform other
families could use.

## The gap this fills

Genealogy platforms (Ancestry, MyHeritage, FamilySearch) record facts: names,
dates, places. They rarely capture the stories, decisions and values behind
those facts. This project aims to preserve both:

- The factual family tree
- The personal stories that bring it to life

## Core components

1. **Personal life story** — childhood, family background, education,
   career, major challenges, decisions, values and lessons. Narrative spine:
   **Values → Challenges → Decisions → Life Path.**
2. **Family history / genealogy** — family tree, historical records
   (including Graham's father's Somerset House research), optional import
   from Ancestry/MyHeritage/FamilySearch.
3. **Multimedia archive** — photos, letters, documents, certificates, voice
   recordings, video. A private digital family museum.
4. **Life timeline** — personal events mapped against world events.
5. **Family collaboration** — relatives invited to add their own memories,
   photos and stories, making the archive multi-perspective.

## Product tiers (for the eventual platform)

- **Standard — "Life Story":** guided questions, voice/text answers, basic
  photo uploads, auto-generated memoir, one printed book.
- **Plus — "Family History":** adds family tree builder, GEDCOM import,
  multi-contributor collaboration, extended archive, multiple printed books.
- **Premium — "Legacy Archive":** AI-guided interviews, permanent
  voice/video storage, searchable archive, interactive timeline, video
  messages for future generations, digital family vault.
- **Future premium feature — AI memory preservation:** a conversational
  archive ("Ask Grandpa") that answers questions using only recorded
  material — never invented content — plus voice recreation from recordings.
  Genuinely futuristic; not needed for the pilot.

Graham's own pilot archive is built as if it were the full Premium tier;
other families could later buy any tier.

## The pilot: Perry Family Archive

Includes Graham's life story, his father's genealogical research, the family
tree, photographs and documents, voice interviews, the UK → Canada →
Australia migration history, and his reflections on values and lessons
learned. This pilot is the proof of concept for everything above.

## Long-term vision

A platform that helps families worldwide preserve personal history and
wisdom, so future generations can explore the lives, stories and values of
those who came before them — not just names and dates.
