# Roles

Keeping roles clear prevents the project becoming overwhelming for anyone.

## Graham — Founder / Story Owner
- Storyteller and historian of his own life
- Curator of family documents, photos and material
- Decides what goes in and what stays out
- Records voice memos and gathers content at his own pace

## Tom — Explorer / Builder
- Sets up and maintains this repo and archive structure
- Explores tools (transcription, AI organisation, future platform tech)
- Evaluates whether/how this becomes a broader business
- Not responsible for producing the life content itself

## Family members — Contributors
- Supply their own photos, letters and memories
- Verify or add context to events Graham recalls
- Optional, added over time via `archive/` contributions

## AI assistant (Claude / ChatGPT) — Editor & Strategist
- Structures the autobiography and suggests interview questions
- Helps organise transcripts into chapters
- Supports planning, roadmap and future platform design
- Never invents content — only works from what's actually recorded
