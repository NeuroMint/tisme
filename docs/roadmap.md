# Roadmap — Pilot (Graham's Autobiography)

Deliberately simple. The objective right now is the autobiography; the
platform/business idea (`docs/concept-brief.md`) stays parked until this
pilot proves the process out.

| Phase | Task | Timing | Soft cost | Hard cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Setup** | Create this repo's folder structure; sketch the Life Timeline Map (10–15 milestones, see `docs/chapter-outline.md`) | ~2 weeks | A few hours of Graham's time | $0 |
| **2. Interviews** | Weekly voice-memo sessions (30–60 min) using `docs/interview-guide.md`; one topic per session | 2–3 months | Graham's time only | $0 |
| **3. Archive building** | Scan/digitise photos and documents into `archive/`; add captions and dates | 1–2 months, can overlap Phase 2 | Sorting time | $100–$300 (scanning service/equipment if needed) |
| **4. Autobiography draft** | Turn transcripts into chapters per `docs/chapter-outline.md`; edit, insert photos | 1 month | Editing time (Tom/AI-assisted) | $0 |
| **5. Printed book** | Layout and print via Blurb/Lulu/KDP | 2–4 weeks | — | $80–$150/copy |
| **6. (Optional) Platform evaluation** | Tom reviews what worked/didn't in Phases 1–5 and decides whether a repeatable product makes sense | Open-ended, after Phase 5 | Tom's time | $0 until a decision is made |

**Estimated pilot total: $200–$500**, mostly scanning and printed copies.

## Working rule

Don't try to do more than one small thing at a time:
- This week: create folders, sketch the timeline.
- Next: record 2–3 memories.
- After that: let documents/photos trigger more stories as they come up.

No deadlines on Phases 2–3 — they can run in parallel and at whatever pace
feels comfortable.
